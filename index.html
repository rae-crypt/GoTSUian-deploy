How to enable your location

Follow the steps below to make sure your pickup location appears correctly on the map.

1

Turn on GPS

Enable Location Services on your device so the browser can find you.

2

Open GoTSUian

Use your browser to load the GoTSUian website and allow location access.

3

Allow access

Grant the prompt so the app can display your exact pickup point.

4

Watch the map

Wait for the map to update — your current location will appear automatically.
