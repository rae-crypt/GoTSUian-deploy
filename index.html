Can't See Your Current Location?

Follow these quick steps to enable location access and get your ride started smoothly.

Step 1: GPS

Turn on your device’s Location Services (GPS).

Step 2: GoTSUian

Open the GoTSUian website in your browser.

Step 3: Allow Access

Allow location access when prompted.

Step 4: Map

Wait for the map to load—your current location will appear automatically.
