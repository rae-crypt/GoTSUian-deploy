GoTSUian Code of Conduct & Terms and Conditions

These rules apply to every student and driver on GoTSUian. Breaking them can result in a warning or a violation on your account, issued by the admin. Together with the Terms and Conditions below, they govern your use of the Service — by creating an account, you agree to both.

For Students / Passengers

Booking a ride means you agree to show up, pay the correct fare, and treat your driver with respect.

Fake or fraudulent booking

Warning

Requesting a ride with no intention of riding — wastes the driver's time and fuel.

No-show at pickup point

Warning

Booking a ride, then not showing up at the pickup location without cancelling.

Harassment or rude behavior toward a driver

Violation

Verbal abuse, threats, or disrespectful treatment of a driver.

Refusing to pay the agreed fare

Violation

Not paying the displayed fare (₱60 Solo / ₱20–35 Shared, split by student count).

Misuse of a Shared ride

Warning

Adding students to a Shared trip who weren't part of the booking or fare split.

False information at registration

Violation

Using a fake name or student number to create an account.

Abuse of the complaint system

Violation

Repeatedly filing false or baseless complaints against drivers.

For Drivers

Accepting a ride means committing to safe driving, fair pricing, and courteous service.

Reckless or unsafe driving

Violation

Safety-critical — dangerous driving is never just a warning.

Overcharging

Violation

Charging more than the fare shown in the app.

Harassment or rude behavior toward a passenger

Violation

Verbal abuse or disrespectful treatment of a passenger.

Unjustified cancellation after accepting

Warning

Accepting a ride, then cancelling it without a valid reason.

Refusing service without valid reason

Violation

Discriminating against or refusing a specific passenger unfairly.

Unsafe vehicle condition

Warning

E.g. faulty brakes, no lights at night — a safety hazard to passengers.

How this works today

Warnings and violations are usually issued manually by the GoTSUian admin — either after reviewing a complaint, or directly if the admin notices a violation themselves. The one exception: a second warning on the same account is automatically issued as a Violation instead — repeat issues are treated more seriously. Your own warning/violation history is visible on your Profile page under "Account Standing."

Eligibility

The Service is intended for use by verified members of the Tarlac State University community:

  • Students — currently enrolled and using a valid TSU student account
  • Drivers — tricycle drivers authorized to operate within the campus service area

You must provide accurate, current, and complete information during registration. Accounts created with false, misleading, or fraudulent information may be suspended or removed.

3

Accounts and Security

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and for all activity that occurs under your account. You agree to notify the GoTSUian administrator immediately of any unauthorized use of your account. GoTSUian is not liable for any loss arising from your failure to safeguard your login details.

4

Description of the Service

GoTSUian is a booking platform that connects students with tricycle drivers within the campus service area. Features include ride booking, shared (carpool) rides with fare splitting, and trip status tracking. GoTSUian facilitates the connection between students and drivers; it does not itself provide transportation and is not a transportation carrier.

5

Fares and Payments

Estimated fares are displayed within the app before a booking is confirmed. Standard fares are ₱60 for a Solo ride and ₱20–₱35 per student for a Shared ride, split according to the number of participating students. All payments are made in cash and settled directly and manually between the student and the driver at the time of the ride. GoTSUian does not process, hold, or handle any payments, and does not support online or electronic payment methods such as e-wallets or bank transfers. The app only displays the fare for reference. Students agree to pay the correct displayed fare in cash, and drivers agree not to charge more than the fare shown in the app.

6

User Conduct

All users agree to behave respectfully and lawfully while using the Service. Specific expectations for students and drivers — and the corresponding warnings and violations — are set out in the Code of Conduct above. Prohibited behavior includes, but is not limited to, fraudulent bookings, harassment, overcharging, unsafe driving, providing false information, and abuse of the complaint system.

7

Warnings, Violations, and Enforcement

GoTSUian uses a two-tier system of Warnings and Violations, as described in the Code of Conduct above. Warnings and violations are generally issued by the GoTSUian administrator, either after reviewing a complaint or upon directly observing a breach. A second warning issued to the same account is automatically escalated to a Violation. Your account standing, including any warnings or violations, is visible on your Profile page. GoTSUian reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts that repeatedly or seriously breach these Terms or the Code of Conduct.

8

Cancellations and No-Shows

Students should cancel a booking promptly if their plans change. Failing to appear at a pickup point without cancelling, or a driver cancelling an accepted ride without a valid reason, may result in a warning or violation as described in the Code of Conduct above.

9

Safety

Safety is a shared responsibility. Drivers agree to operate their vehicles safely, keep them in roadworthy condition, and comply with applicable traffic rules. Students agree to conduct themselves safely during trips. Any safety-critical breach, such as reckless driving or an unsafe vehicle condition, is treated with the highest seriousness.
