Code of Conduct & Terms

GoTSUian Code of Conduct & Terms and Conditions

These rules apply to every student and driver on GoTSUian. Breaking them can result in a warning or a violation on your account, issued by the admin. Together with the Terms and Conditions below, they govern your use of the Service — by creating an account, you agree to both.

For Students / Passengers

Booking a ride means you agree to show up, pay the correct fare, and treat your driver with respect.

Fake or fraudulent booking

Warning

Requesting a ride with no intention of riding — wastes the driver's time and fuel.

No-show at pickup point

Warning

Booking a ride, then not showing up at the pickup location without cancelling.

Harassment or rude behavior toward a driver

Violation

Verbal abuse, threats, or disrespectful treatment of a driver.

Refusing to pay the agreed fare

Violation

Not paying the fare shown in the app, which is calculated from the road distance between your pickup and your drop-off.

Misuse of a Shared ride

Warning

Adding students to a Shared trip who weren't part of the booking or fare split.

False information at registration

Violation

Using a fake name or student number to create an account.

Abuse of the complaint system

Violation

Repeatedly filing false or baseless complaints against drivers.

For Drivers

Accepting a ride means committing to safe driving, fair pricing, and courteous service.

Reckless or unsafe driving

Violation

Safety-critical — dangerous driving is never just a warning.

Overcharging

Violation

Charging more than the fare shown in the app.

Harassment or rude behavior toward a passenger

Violation

Verbal abuse or disrespectful treatment of a passenger.

Unjustified cancellation after accepting

Warning

Accepting a ride, then cancelling it without a valid reason.

Refusing service without valid reason

Violation

Discriminating against or refusing a specific passenger unfairly.

Unsafe vehicle condition

Warning

E.g. faulty brakes, no lights at night — a safety hazard to passengers.

How this works today

Warnings and violations are usually issued manually by the GoTSUian admin — either after reviewing a complaint, or directly if the admin notices a violation themselves. The one exception: a second warning on the same account is automatically issued as a Violation instead — repeat issues are treated more seriously. A Violation suspends the account: you are logged out and can't log in again until the GoTSUian admin lifts the suspension. Your own warning/violation history is visible on your Profile page under "Account Standing."

Description of the Service

GoTSUian is a booking platform that connects passengers with tricycle drivers. Features include ride booking from the passenger's current location, distance-based fare estimates, live driver tracking with an estimated time of arrival, in-app chat between the passenger and the driver, and trip status tracking. GoTSUian facilitates the connection between passengers and drivers; it does not itself provide transportation and is not a transportation carrier.

5

Fares and Payments

Fares are based on the distance of the trip and follow Tarlac City Ordinance IX-4-001-2024 (student rate). A ride costs ₱20 for the first kilometer plus ₱5 for each additional kilometer, and a started kilometer counts as a whole one. The distance is measured automatically along the road, from the passenger's pickup point (their current location) to the drop-off they choose. The estimated fare is shown in the app before a booking is confirmed, and the same fare is shown to the driver. The rates may be updated by the GoTSUian administrator in line with the city ordinance; the fare shown at the time of booking is the one that applies to that ride. Because pickup and drop-off points are found using automated location services, the measured distance may occasionally be approximate.

All payments are made in cash and settled directly between the passenger and the driver at the end of the ride. GoTSUian does not process, hold, or handle any payments, and does not support online or electronic payment methods such as e-wallets or bank transfers. The app only displays the fare for reference. Passengers agree to pay the fare shown in the app, and drivers agree not to charge more than that fare.

A passenger may book a ride for someone else, such as a family member, by setting a different pickup point and giving that person's name and mobile number. The account holder who made the booking remains responsible for the ride, its fare, and the conduct of the person riding.

Privacy Policy

GoTSUian respects your privacy and handles personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). This section explains what we collect, why, and who can see it.

1

What we collect

  • Account details: your full name, email address and mobile number. Your password is stored encrypted (hashed); no one, including the administrator, can see it.
  • Driver details: driver's license number, a photo of the license, and tricycle plate number, used only to verify drivers before they can accept rides.
  • Ride details: pickup and drop-off locations, fare, distance, ride status and times.
  • Location: your pickup point is taken from your phone's GPS when you book. A driver's live location is shared only during an active ride, so the passenger can track the trip. A passenger's live location is never sent to our server.
  • Booking for someone else: the name and mobile number of the person you book a ride for, shown only to the driver of that ride so they can call them.
  • Messages, ratings and reports: in-ride chat between a passenger and their driver, reviews, and complaints you submit.
2

How we use it

To match passengers with drivers, show fares and arrival times, verify drivers, handle complaints and violations, and send verification codes by email. We do not sell, rent or share your information with advertisers or other third parties.

3

Who can see it

Your driver sees your name and your pickup and drop-off points only for rides they accept. Passengers see a driver's name and plate number. The GoTSUian administrator can see account, ride and complaint records in order to manage the Service.

4

Third-party services

Maps, place search and road routes come from OpenStreetMap services. They receive map locations, not your name or account, in order to draw routes and find places.

5

Your rights

You may ask to view the personal information GoTSUian holds about you by emailing gotsuian.system@gmail.com.
