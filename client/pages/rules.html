Code of Conduct

GoTSUian Code of Conduct

These rules apply to every student and driver on GoTSUian. Breaking them can result in a warning or a violation on your account, issued by the admin.

Warning Violation

For Students / Passengers

Booking a ride means you agree to show up, pay the correct fare, and treat your driver with respect.

Violation Severity Explanation
Fake or fraudulent booking Warning Requesting a ride with no intention of riding — wastes the driver's time and fuel.
No-show at pickup point Warning Booking a ride, then not showing up at the pickup location without cancelling.
Harassment or rude behavior toward a driver Violation Verbal abuse, threats, or disrespectful treatment of a driver.
Refusing to pay the agreed fare Violation Not paying the displayed fare (₱60 Solo / ₱20–35 Shared, split by student count).
Misuse of a Shared ride Warning Adding students to a Shared trip who weren't part of the booking or fare split.
False information at registration Violation Using a fake name or student number to create an account.
Abuse of the complaint system Violation Repeatedly filing false or baseless complaints against drivers.

For Drivers

Accepting a ride means committing to safe driving, fair pricing, and courteous service.

Violation Severity Explanation
Reckless or unsafe driving Violation Safety-critical — dangerous driving is never just a warning.
Overcharging Violation Charging more than the fare shown in the app.
Harassment or rude behavior toward a passenger Violation Verbal abuse or disrespectful treatment of a passenger.
Unjustified cancellation after accepting Warning Accepting a ride, then cancelling it without a valid reason.
Refusing service without valid reason Violation Discriminating against or refusing a specific passenger unfairly.
Unsafe vehicle condition Warning E.g. faulty brakes, no lights at night — a safety hazard to passengers.

How this works today

Warnings and violations are usually issued manually by the GoTSUian admin — either after reviewing a complaint, or directly if the admin notices a violation themselves. The one exception: a second warning on the same account is automatically issued as a Violation instead — repeat issues are treated more seriously. Your own warning/violation history is visible on your Profile page under "Account Standing."
